# Lunar Nakshatra Compatibility Screening Rules (`koota-policy`)

> Rules for Synastry Layer 1 ("Nakshatra & Fundamental Compatibility").
> Stance: KN Rao's authoritative principle — Ashtakoota serves exclusively as a preliminary lunar **screening layer**, never an absolute arbiter and never reduced to an arbitrary X/36 total score.
> Data Source: Both natal charts' Nakshatra tables (Moon Nakshatra/Pada) and Moon sign lords from `structured_data.md`.

---

## 0. Canonical Principles

- **Method**: Standard North Indian Eight-Koota framework (Guna Milan) used for foundational screening.
- **No Composite Score Out of 36**: Mapped into modern relational dimensions.
- **Doshas Do Not Possess Veto Power**: A dosha (such as Nadi or Bhakoot) flags an area requiring conscious navigation; final structural endurance is governed by D1/D9 7th house integrity and Dasha concurrency (Layers 0, 2, and 3).
