# Birth Time Calibration Questionnaire Specification (`calibration_quiz`)

> Read When: A layer (D1 / D9 / D10 / D4 / D5 / 3d Nakshatra boundary) has low diagnostic confidence and requires targeted behavioral/event differentiation. **Read fully before drafting questions**.
> Role: Supplements the mapping judgment table in `SKILL.md`; does not add unvetted phases; maximum 2 rounds per layer.

---

## 0. Why Questions Fail to Differentiate

When questions fail to separate candidates, it is almost always due to one of the following root causes (addressed by the 7-step process):
1. **Generic barnum statements**: Asking whether someone is "steady" or "adaptable" applies to everyone.
2. **Illusory differences**: The candidates do not actually differ in that astrological domain.
3. **Asymmetric options**: One option sounds significantly more socially desirable.
4. **General baseline bias**: Options reflect general generational norms rather than chart dynamics.
5. **Multi-axial conflation**: Packing multiple disparate questions into a single item.
6. **Unanswerable prompts**: Inquiring about subjective internal states or obscure memories.
7. **Post-facto mapping**: Altering option-to-candidate mappings after reading the user's answers.

---

## 1. Entry Conditions

- **Map First; Inquire Only When Indeterminate**: The layer mapping table, primary hypothesis, and counter-arguments must be drafted first.
- Clearly identify the unresolved candidate Ascendants and state how each question could decisively pivot the evaluation.
- **Ask Objective Facts Directly**: When unstated historical facts differ between candidates (education degree, relocation count, family structure, major health events), ask the direct fact rather than framing an ambiguous A/B quiz.
- Do not ask for arbitrary precision (months/days) unless a critical Dasha transition boundary falls within that specific interval.

---

## 2. Locking Comparison Targets

- D1 questions compare distinct rising signs. Different time intervals sharing the same rising sign share the identical structural mapping.
- D9/D10/D4/D5 questions compare differing internal divisional structures.
- All option mappings must be locked prior to receiving user answers.

## 3. Seven Steps for Drafting Calibration Questions

### Step 1: Inventory Genuine Astrological Differences
Identify features that genuinely fluctuate between candidate charts:
- D1: Lagna lord house, house lords, kendra occupants (1/4/7/10).
- D9: D9 Lagna lord, 7th house and 7th lord, Venus/DK placements.
- D10: D10 Lagna lord, 10th house occupants, Sun/Saturn/AmK placements.
- Sort differences by structural strength (Strong vs. Weak).

### Step 2: Establish Opposing Observable Manifestations
For each major difference, write concrete, observable behavioral/circumstantial manifestations:
- Same operational domain and same granularity (e.g., both describing leadership under crisis).
- Focus on external actions, social perceptions, and real-world occurrences rather than internal self-image.
- State why Candidate X will manifest as Option A and why Candidate Y will manifest as Option B.

### Step 3: Select the Most Objective Question Format
In order of preference:
1. **Direct Objective Fact** (verifiable historical fact).
2. **Behavioral Experience** (concrete past action during a specific crisis/turning point).
3. **Third-Party Perception** (how close colleagues or family consistently perceive you).
4. **Abstract Preference** (least preferred; prone to social desirability bias).

### Step 4: Construct Symmetrical A / B / 0 Choices
- Neutral wording of comparable length and tone.
- Option 0 = Both equally, neither, or situational.
- Alternate candidate assignments between A and B across consecutive questions.

### Step 5: The Three Mandatory Quality Checks
1. **Inversion Check**: If Candidate X and Y mappings are swapped, does the logic hold? If yes -> Zero diagnostic value -> Discard.
2. **Single-Axis Check**: Does the question isolate exactly one astrological variable?
3. **Answerability Check**: Can the user answer accurately without speculative introspection?

### Step 6: Batching and Pre-Commitment
Group questions from independent astrological variables into batches of 2-3 items. Lock mappings into the working log before presenting to the user.

### Step 7: Post-Response Evaluation
Incorporate valid answers back into the analytical mapping ledger. Never score questions as mechanical points; assess them as qualitative astrological evidence.
