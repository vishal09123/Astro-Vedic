# Romantic Relationship Framework (`romantic-framework`)

> Scope: Romantic partnerships, dating, formal marriage.
> Invocation: Selected during intake after Layer 0.5 neutral screening.
> Gender Differentiation:
> - Heterosexual + Disclosed Gender: Fully integrates classical gender Karakas (Jupiter = Husband in female chart; Venus = Wife in male chart), combined with 7th house, DK, and UL.
> - Non-Binary / Same-Sex / Undisclosed Gender: Analyzed directionally via Person A <-> Person B + Darakaraka (DK) + Venus (relationship significator).

---

## Layer 0 Individual Capacity: Core Metrics

- **D1 Focus**: 5th house (romance), 7th house and lord (committed partnership), Venus (love/attraction), Moon (emotional needs), Darakaraka (DK), Upapada Lagna (UL).
- **D9 Navamsa**: Ascendant, 7th house and lord, Venus, DK (soul-level partnership quality).
- **Dasha Timing**: Activation of 5th, 7th, 8th, 11th, or 12th houses, Venus, DK, or UL.

## Layer 2 Inter-Chart Overlay Signals

- Magnetism & Intimacy: Venus-to-Mars, Moon-to-Venus, Venus-to-Jupiter; Mars aspecting counterpart's 5th, 7th, or 8th house.
- 8th and 12th Houses: House 8 governs Mangalya (marital endurance); House 12 governs Shayana (private intimacy); evaluate both constructive and friction dimensions.
- Structural Endurance: Saturn aspecting Moon, Venus, 7th house, or UL; Jupiter protecting 7th house or UL.
- Karmic Fascination: Rahu-Ketu axis contacting Moon, Venus, Lagna, or 7th house.
