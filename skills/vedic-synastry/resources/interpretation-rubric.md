# Six-Dimensional Assessment Rubric (`interpretation-rubric`)

> Synthesis guidelines for final synastry reporting. Employs a qualitative Six-Dimensional Matrix **without reducing relationship to an arbitrary single score**.
> Framework: KN Rao / Parashari synthesis.

---

## 0. Evidence Weight Hierarchy

`D1 Directional House Overlays > UL / DK Mutual Triggers > D9 Navamsa Capacity > Dasha Timing Alignment > Ashtakoota Lunar Screening`.
No single metric possesses absolute veto power; however, if Layer 0 identifies a severe deficiency in baseline individual capacity, the overall assessment is flagged as **Conditional**.

---

## 1. Dimensional Evaluation Tiers

| Dimension | Tiers | Key Indicators |
|-----------|-------|----------------|
| **Emotional Safety** | Supportive / Mixed / High Stress | Supportive: Harmonious Moon-to-Moon, Jupiter/Venus aspecting Moon; High Stress: Saturn-Moon compression, Rahu-Moon volatility, malefics in 4th house. |
| **Attraction & Intimacy** | Gentle / Intense / Asymmetric / Fixation Risk | Gentle: Moon-Venus, Venus-Jupiter; Intense: Close Venus-Mars, Mars aspecting 5th/7th/8th; Asymmetric: One-sided overlays; Fixation: Rahu contacting Venus/Moon. |
| **Communication & Repair** | Fluid / Navigable / Escalation-Prone | Fluid: Harmonious Mercury-Mercury/Moon; Escalation-Prone: Mars aspecting Mercury, afflicted 3rd/2nd houses. |
| **Long-Term Endurance** | Resourced / Conditional / Structural Fragility | Resourced: Harmonious Saturn support to 7th house/UL, Jupiter protection, robust D9; Fragility: Severe afflictions without benefic rescue. |
| **Practical Collaboration** | Complementary / Competitive / Imbalanced Load | Complementary: Productive 10th/11th/2nd house overlays, AmK harmony; Competitive: Unresolved Mars-Mars or Sun-Sun tensions. |
| **Current Timing Alignment**| Synchronized / Misaligned / Delay Recommended | Synchronized: Simultaneous activation of relationship houses/significators across both Dashas. |
