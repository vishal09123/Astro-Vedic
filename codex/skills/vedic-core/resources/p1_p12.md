# P1–P12 Planetary Audit Parameters Definition

> Origin: KN Rao System (Parashari framework).
> Purpose: Methodological guideline for evaluating individual grahas sequentially from P1 through P12.
>
> ⚠️ **Qualitative Scale**: Percentage weights (e.g., P2 Vargottama x 150%, P10 Mars +20%, P12 +0.2) represent qualitative prioritization guidelines rather than literal mathematical metrics; articulate them in prose as "stabilized", "attenuated", or "requiring conscious exertion".

---

## P1 Functional Role [Critical Priority]

The functional agenda of a planet is governed strictly by the houses it rules:
- **Core-Driver (1st Lord)**: Personal champion; directly serves querent's identity and vitality.
- **Yogakaraka (Ruling a Kendra and a Trikona concurrently)**: Highest structural benefactor.
- **Faithful (5th and 9th Lords)**: Trikona lords; natural auspicious allies.
- **Trader (2nd, 4th, 7th, 10th Lords)**: Kendradhipati dynamic: benefics ruling kendras temper passive ease; malefics ruling kendras cultivate constructive discipline.
- **Growth-Hacker (3rd, 6th, 11th Lords)**: Upachaya lords; growth achieved through intense competitive exertion.
- **Destroyer (8th and 12th Lords)**: Dusthana lords; specialists in termination, research, auditing, and foreign affairs.

### P1.3 Functional Texture (Mandatory Audit)
- **Soft-Betrayal**: Natural benefics (Jupiter, Venus, Mercury, Moon) serving as Growth-Hackers or Destroyers -> well-intentioned over-expansion or indulgence leading to depletion.
- **Hard-Construction**: Natural malefics (Saturn, Mars, Rahu, Ketu) serving as Core-Drivers or Yogakarakas -> strenuous labor yielding enduring, tangible achievements.

### P1.4 Maraka Context (Applied Strictly to Vitality/Health Inquiries)
- Governed by the 2nd and 7th lords.
- Operative primarily when evaluating physical vitality and health crises during active Dashas. In professional and financial contexts, these lords function as standard commercial traders.

## P2 Planetary Health & Vitality [Significant Priority]

- **Combustion (Separation from Sun)**:
  Moon: 12° | Mars: 17° | Mercury: 14° (12° Retrograde) | Jupiter: 11° | Venus: 10° (8° Retrograde) | Saturn: 15°.
  Cazimi (< 1°) remains functionally afflicted per KN Rao.
- **Retrograde Motion**:
  Natural benefics retrograde: Deep intellectual introspection; internalization.
  Natural malefics retrograde: Recurring historical obstacles; persistent tenacity.
  Retrograde lords signify delays or iterative reviews in governed house affairs.
- **Planetary War (Graha Yuddha)**: Separation < 1° between classical grahas (excluding Sun, Moon, nodes). Victor = lower longitude (or higher Shadbala); defeated planet suffers functional paralysis.
- **Vargottama**: Occupying the same sign in D1 and D9 -> structural integrity fortified.
- **Dig Bala (Directional Strength)**:
  Jupiter / Mercury in House 1; Moon / Venus in House 4; Saturn in House 7; Sun / Mars in House 10.

## P3 Multi-House Coupling & Conjunctions [Reference Priority]

- **Dual House Rulership**: Intertwines the affairs of both governed houses.
- **Conjunction Proximity Tiers**:
  - `< 3°`: Intimate fusion; full mutual modification.
  - `3° - 10°`: Standard classical conjunction.
  - `> 10°`: Loose whole-sign association; halved intensity.
- **Parivartana (Mutual Reception)**: Exchange of signs between two house lords creates a mutual operational bridge.

## P5 Environmental Road Conditions (Resident House) [Significant Priority]

- Auspicious Houses (1, 4, 5, 7, 9, 10): Minimal resistance; smooth expression.
- 6th House: 50% baseline efficiency (competitive, service-heavy arena).
- 8th House: 30% baseline efficiency (transformative, crisis-laden arena).
- 12th House: 20% baseline efficiency (expenditure, seclusion, cross-border arena).
- **Viparita Raja Yoga (VRY)**: Dusthana lords (6, 8, 12) occupying other dusthanas without benefic conjunctions -> resilience forged through adversity.

## P7 Essential Dignity & Neecha Bhanga [Critical Priority]

- Exalted -> Own Sign / Moolatrikona -> Great Friend / Friend -> Neutral -> Enemy / Great Enemy -> Debilitated.
- **Neecha Bhanga (Cancellation of Debilitation)**:
  Read directly from `structured_data.md` Neecha Bhanga table. Multiple cancellations mitigate degradation into an arduous struggle with an ultimate safety net; a single cancellation notes partial relief without erasing baseline friction.
- **Uchcha Bhanga (Afflicted Exaltation)**:
  Exalted planets conjoined/aspected by malefics, combust, or with an afflicted dispositor are flagged as having high potential hindered by structural constraints.

## P7.4 Nodes (Rahu & Ketu) Evaluation Standard

The nodes have no house rulerships, essential dignities, or Shadbala. Evaluated via:
1. **Dispositor Strength**: Sign lord of the node's resident sign.
2. **House Nature**: Favorable in kendras, trikonas, or Upachaya houses (3, 11 for Rahu).
3. **Close Conjunctions (< 5°)**: Natural benefics channel and guide energy; natural malefics amplify disruptive volatility.

## P8 Planetary Age & Degree Status (Avasthas) [Auxiliary Priority]

- Infant (0°-6°): Nascent potential.
- Youth (6°-12°): Emerging development.
- Prime (12°-18°): Peak autonomous agency.
- Mature (18°-24°): Stable, seasoned execution.
- Aged (24°-30°): Diminished physical vitality; supportive wisdom.

## Conflict Resolution Protocols

1. **Benefic Lord in Dusthana vs. High Dignity**: "High-value asset operating under demanding operational constraints."
2. **Dusthana Placement + High BAV**: "Resourceful operator excelling in complex environments."
3. **Directional Indicator (Karaka) vs. Planetary Affliction**: Karaka dictates vocational direction; planetary affliction reflects operational friction during execution. Never use operational friction to negate directional alignment.
4. **Domain Activation != Event Manifestation != Timing Window**: Separate domain relevance (house/Karaka) from structural manifestation (dignity/aspects) and chronological timing (Dasha/transit).
