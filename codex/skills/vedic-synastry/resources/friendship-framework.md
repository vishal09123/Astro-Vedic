# Friendship and Peer Framework (`friendship-framework`)

> Scope: Platonic friends, intellectual companions, creative peers.
> Invocation: Selected during intake after Layer 0.5 neutral screening.
> Gender: Non-gendered framework, evaluated strictly directionally.

---

## Layer 0 Individual Capacity: Core Metrics

- **D1 Focus**: 3rd house (peers/camaraderie), 11th house (social networks/shared ideals), Moon (emotional rapport), Mercury (conversation), Lagna (temperamental harmony).
- **Dasha Timing**: Whether active Dashas stimulate 3rd or 11th houses.

## Layer 2 Inter-Chart Overlay Signals

- Emotional Resonance: Moon-to-Moon, Moon-to-Mercury.
- Intellectual Alignment: Mercury-to-Mercury, Jupiter contacts (shared ethics/worldview).
- Social Synergies: 3rd and 11th house mutual overlays.
- Good Faith & Goodwill: Jupiter cross-aspects.
- Friction Points: Mars-to-Mercury, 6th/8th/12th house overlays.
- Sexual tension indicators (Venus-Mars) are omitted from platonic evaluation.

## Six-Dimensional Priority Matrix

- **Primary Weights**: Emotional Safety, Communication & Repair, Practical Collaboration (shared pursuits).
- "Attraction & Intimacy" is reinterpreted as "Natural Affinity & Camaraderie".
