# Prashna Judgment Sheet Writing Guide

> **Purpose**: Governs Phase 5 generation of the final human-readable judgment sheet, articulating chart findings in a compassionate, professional astrological voice.
> **Authority**: This document dictates delivery and phrasing only; it **does not score points and cannot alter outcome tiers**. Directional orientation is determined exclusively by the judgment script (`prashna_verdict.md`, `judgment-rubric.md`). House, planetary, and sign meanings serve narrative explanation only and never alter ledger totals.

---

## 1. Tone and Scope

- **Astrological Demeanor**: Point directly to concrete chart placements first (planet, house, dignity, aspect), cite the classical perspective, and translate into the querent's real-world circumstances.
- **Classical Terminology**: Authentic Sanskrit terms (Lagna, Bhava, Graha, Drishti, Kendra, Trikona, Neecha, Chara/Sthira, Shirshodaya, and external/internal house names per `house-meanings.md` §2) are encouraged. **Every technical term must be followed immediately by a plain-language English explanation upon its first appearance**.
- **Original Phrasing**: Avoid rigid boilerplate or fill-in-the-blank monotony. Two judgments in the same category must read like bespoke, nuanced analyses.
- **Section Length**: Each subsection contains 2–4 paragraphs tied directly to chart placements. The client-facing narrative typically spans 1,500–3,000 words. Topic queries with fewer active rules should not be artificially padded, but every subsection must be substantively developed. Descriptive queries require ~1,000 words (§7).
- **Constructive Ethics**: Never predict death, lifespan, or terminal tragedy. Evaluate the 8th house strictly per context (`house-meanings.md` §7): in private inquiries it signifies seclusion/confidentiality; in general queries it signifies structural friction or hidden delays. Saturn represents burdens, fatigue, or delays, never fatalism.
- **Omit Input Sensitivity**: Do not mention input sensitivity or suggest "if the time were more precise" in the client-facing section; technical time-shift audits belong exclusively to the collapsible audit section.
- **Omit Technical Slugs**: Never speak internal mechanical slugs or status codes (such as Favorable, Unfavorable, Balanced, `rule_id`, or internal code variables) directly to the user. Express direction naturally through prose.

## 3. Composing "What the Chart Indicates"

**The Success Ledger is the Skeleton of the Narrative**: The narrative articulates the exact reasoning that produced the tier. Every scoring rule must be covered, organized into supportive forces, frictional resistances, and neutral factors. Omitted or contextually excluded entries must not be cited as operative evidence.

1. **Section Headers Named by Chart Features**: Use evocative, specific titles (e.g. "Gemini Rising: An Airy Sign Lacking Grounding", "The Seventh House: Saturn's Heavy Gaze"), avoiding empty labels like "Your Side".
2. **Three Dimensions Covered**:
   - **Ability** (`judgment-rubric.md` §5): Can the matter lord fulfill its promises? Discuss afflictions, mitigations, and capacity adjustments.
   - **Desire (Planetary Engagement)** (`judgment-rubric.md` §6):
     1. Identity: The planet's natural Karaka and houses ruled;
     2. Venue: Significations of the occupied house;
     3. Companions: Conjoined and aspecting planets;
     4. Dignity: Retrograde, combustion, debilitation described as operational modes;
     5. Bridges: Connection to querent via aspects, kendras, or planetary bridges.
   - **Environment**: Support and afflictions around the Lagna and Matter House, lunar state, and the combined weight of classical rules.
3. **The 3-Step Movement for Every Fact**: Concrete chart placement -> Classical authority -> Real-world translation.
4. **Separate Background Facts from Active Evidence**: Use phrases like "Classical texts state..." for active ledger rules; use "Additionally, from the background..." for supporting facts without drawing independent conclusions. Background facts opposing the verdict belong strictly in "Turning Points" or "Reservations" without overriding the ledger.
5. **Harmonious Direction**: Title, summary, and narrative body must align perfectly with the internal tier.
6. **Prominent Chart Features**: If the user asked about a specific planet, or if prominent exalted planets/Moon appear, address them directly in a brief subsection explaining their classical role and why they do or do not score points in this query.
7. **Action Queries Addressed in Two Distinct Steps**: Clearly distinguish whether the person will initiate action (Action Chain) from whether the event will succeed (Success Ledger).

## 5. Client Narrative Contract

1. **No Bare Tiers or Labels**: Never write "favorable/unfavorable", raw code slugs, or fatalistic words ("destined", "impossible", "guaranteed").
2. **Blind Test**: Readers must discern the directional orientation purely from the title, summary, and narrative tone.
3. **Ledger Grounding**: Every evaluative statement must correspond directly to an entry in the Success Ledger.
4. **Authorized Meanings**: Interpretations draw strictly from authorized sources (§2).
5. **Jargon Decoded**: Every technical term is defined immediately in plain English.
6. **No Stereotyped Boilerplate**: Phrased with organic, natural fluency.
7. **Turning Points**: Favorable with obstacles or Unfavorable with turning points must identify the exact opposing ledger entries.
8. **Balanced Verdicts**: Clearly specify the competing factors and the pivotal signal that will break the tie.
9. **Unfavorable Verdicts**: Do not deliver dead-end pronouncements; explain current blockages, constructive current steps, and what realistic changes warrant a fresh inquiry.
10. **Timing Integration**: Synthesize primary timing and nearest trigger dates into a cohesive sentence; withhold timing when the verdict is Unfavorable.

---

## 6. Supportive Communication Guidelines

For emotionally vulnerable relational inquiries (reconciliation, breakups, silence):
1. Offer empathetic support once, concretely referencing the user's situation, avoiding hollow clichés ("everything happens for a reason", "let go to receive").
2. Do not repeat emotional consolations in subsequent Q&A unless the user expresses renewed distress.
3. Consolation never alters the verdict.
4. Ground reassurance in chart facts: point to turning points or conditions for renewed inquiry.
5. Inquiries involving manipulation or surveillance: explain neutrally why horary rejects such inquiries and offer an alternative constructive framing.
6. Self-harm or crisis remarks: prioritize immediate safety resources; do not cast charts.
