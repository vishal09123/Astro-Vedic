# Yoga Assessment Methodology

> Purpose: Reference for `vedic-core` during Section 10 ("Yoga Assessment").
> Core Principle: Articulate potential, structural channels, and strategic leverage without fatalism.
>
> ⚠️ **Inter-planetary Relations (Mutual Aspect / Reception)**:
> - **Mutual Aspect (Mutual Graha Drishti)**: Dual bidirectional gaze (Planet A aspects House of B AND Planet B aspects House of A). Read directly from `structured_data.md` mutual aspect table. Unidirectional gaze does not constitute a full Yoga channel.
> - **Mutual Reception (Parivartana)**: Exchange of signs between two house lords. Read directly from `structured_data.md` mutual reception table (Maha, Khala, Dainya).
> - **Conjunction**: Occupying the same whole sign / rashi. Degree proximity tiers follow the standard PAC framework.

## Major Classical Yogas Registry

> ⚠️ **Structural Presence**: Verify active status directly from the `Yoga Pre-Scan` table in `structured_data.md`. The structural conditions below define the geometry; model responsibility focuses on **potency assessment, combustion/retrograde degradation, cancellation dynamics, and qualitative synthesis**.

### Dharma-Karma Adhipati Yoga
```
Condition: Conjunction, mutual aspect, or parivartana between 9th lord and 10th lord.
Significance: Innate sense of vocation, ethical leadership, public honor.
Assessment:
  Strong participants (Shadbala > 120%, favorable dignity) -> Natural alignment with professional purpose and social respect.
  Weak participants -> Latent potential requiring activation during operational Dasha periods.
```

### Dhana Yoga
```
Condition: Conjunction, mutual aspect, or parivartana between 2nd and 11th lords,
           or between 2nd/11th lords and 1st, 5th, or 9th lords.
Significance: Capacity for capital accumulation and commercial realization.
Assessment:
  Primary benchmark is the Shadbala and dignity of participating grahas.
  House 2 and House 11 SAV scores serve as resource context only when reaching extreme values (>32 or <20).
```

### Raja Yoga
```
Condition: Combination between a Trikona lord (1st, 5th, 9th) and a Kendra lord (1st, 4th, 7th, 10th).
           (Includes Parivartana between Kendra and Trikona lords).
Significance: Authority, executive agency, social prominence.
Assessment:
  Purity: Evaluate whether participating lords concurrently rule dusthanas (6, 8, 12).
  Strength: Evaluated via Shadbala and Navamsa stability.
```

### Viparita Raja Yoga (VRY)
```
Condition: Lords of dusthanas (6, 8, 12) occupying other dusthanas (6, 8, 12).
           (Verified via structured_data.md VRY active field; mutual reception between dusthana lords is the strongest variant).
Significance: Resilience through crisis; breakthroughs emerging from structural adversity or institutional displacement.
Assessment:
  Requires isolation from benefic house lord conjunctions to maintain purity.
  Never romanticize: VRY manifests only through Navigating tangible obstacles.
```

### Gajakesari Yoga
```
Condition: Jupiter in a Kendra (1st, 4th, 7th, 10th) from the Moon.
Significance: Wisdom, emotional resilience, strategic pedagogical intelligence.
Assessment:
  Shadbala of both Jupiter and Moon dictates actualization scale.
```

### Adhi Yoga
```
Condition: Natural benefics (Jupiter, Venus, Mercury) occupying the 6th, 7th, and 8th houses from the Moon, unblemished by natural malefics.
Significance: Eminent leadership, ministerial authority, enduring executive standing.
Assessment:
  All three houses occupied by unblemished benefics -> Complete, peak efficacy.
  Only 1-2 houses occupied -> Partial manifestation.
  Combustion or debilitation modifies baseline potency.
```

### Chandra-Mangala Yoga
```
Condition: Conjunction or mutual aspect between Moon and Mars.
Significance: Enterprise, decisive commercial momentum, high financial drive; potential emotional volatility.
```

### Kemadruma Yoga
```
Condition: No planets in the 2nd and 12th houses from the Moon (excluding Sun and nodes).
Significance: Psychological isolation, internal detachment, perceived lack of emotional baseline.
Cancellation:
  - Moon in a Kendra (1st, 4th, 7th, 10th from Lagna).
  - Conjunction with another planet.
  - Aspect from Jupiter.
```

### Pancha Mahapurusha Yogas
```
Condition: Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, or Saturn in a Kendra (1st, 4th, 7th, 10th from Lagna) while exalted or in own sign:
  - Ruchaka Yoga (Mars in Kendra in Aries, Scorpio, or Capricorn) -> Courage, command, technical initiative.
  - Bhadra Yoga (Mercury in Kendra in Gemini or Virgo) -> Commercial genius, analytical precision, eloquence.
  - Hamsa Yoga (Jupiter in Kendra in Cancer, Sagittarius, or Pisces) -> Spiritual discernment, wisdom, high ethics.
  - Malavya Yoga (Venus in Kendra in Taurus, Libra, or Pisces) -> Artistic refinement, luxury, diplomatic magnetism.
  - Shasha Yoga (Saturn in Kendra in Libra, Capricorn, or Aquarius) -> Organizational discipline, administrative power, endurance.
```

### Guru Chandala Yoga
```
Condition: Conjunction of Jupiter and Rahu.
Significance: Unconventional philosophies, disruption of orthodox dogma, breakthrough through non-traditional means; risk of distorted judgment if unanchored.
```

### Shakata Yoga
```
Condition: Moon in the 6th, 8th, or 12th house from Jupiter.
Significance: Cyclical financial and emotional fluctuations.
Cancellation: Jupiter or Moon in Kendra from Lagna, or Jupiter exalted/own sign.
```

### Parivartana Yoga (Mutual Reception)
```
Condition: Exchange of signs between two house lords.
Classification (Phaladeepika VI.32-34; 66 total pairs):
  - Maha Parivartana: Exchanges among auspicious houses (1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 9, 10, 11; 28 pairs) -> Mutually reinforcing excellence.
  - Khala Parivartana: 3rd lord exchanging with auspicious houses (8 pairs) -> Irregular cycles, success earned through friction.
  - Dainya Parivartana: Lord of 6, 8, or 12 exchanging with any house (30 pairs) -> Entanglement in complex trials, dual-edged outcomes.
```

## Evaluation Framework

1. **Active Verification**: Confirm presence in `structured_data.md`.
2. **Potency Assessment**:
   - Active + Strong Shadbala (> 120%) = High realization probability.
   - Active + Moderate Shadbala (100-120%) = Latent potential awakened during operational Dashas.
   - Active + Afflicted (combust, debilitated without cancellation) = Structural drag; gains tempered by friction.
3. **Activation Window**: Cross-examine current and upcoming Vimshottari Mahadasha/Antardasha periods of participating grahas.
