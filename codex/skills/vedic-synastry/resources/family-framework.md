# Family and Intergenerational Framework (`family-framework`)

> Scope: Parent-child, siblings, nuclear and extended family synastry.
> Invocation: Selected during intake after Layer 0.5 neutral screening.
> Directionality: Structured directionally (Senior -> Junior / Junior -> Senior, or Person A <-> Person B).

---

## Layer 0 Individual Capacity: Core Metrics

- **D1 Focus**: 4th house (mother/home/emotional baseline), 9th house (father/dharma/lineage), Moon (maternal bond/mind), Sun (paternal authority/ego), 5th house (progeny), Lagna.
- **Children Significator (Karaka)**: Jupiter.
- **Dasha Timing**: Whether active Dashas stimulate 4th, 9th, or 5th houses.

## Layer 2 Inter-Chart Overlay Signals

- Emotional Bonding & Care: Moon inter-chart overlays, 4th house cross-activation.
- Authority & Lineage Guidance: Sun inter-chart aspects, 9th house cross-activation.
- Intergenerational Tension: Saturn aspecting counterpart's Moon, Sun, or 4th house (emotional distance, heavy expectations).
- Protection: Jupiter aspecting counterpart's Lagna or 5th house.
- Karmic Enmeshment: Rahu-Ketu axis contacting Sun, Moon, or 4th house.
